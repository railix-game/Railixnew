Correct order: (1) deploy the RAIL token first, (2) deploy this NFT contract with the token address, (3) owner signs setMinter on the token page with this NFT address, (4) owner signs setMintEnabled(true) here, (5) only then can the public use mintFree, mintFreeFee and mintPaid. Each NFT minted also grants 1,000 RAIL automatically. Funds stay in the contract until the owner presses Withdraw below.

Prices locked in the contract: Phase 1 #1–#444 free mint 0 BNB (max 3 per mint); after #444, free mint + 0.0001 BNB fee (max 10 per mint) AND paid mint 0.0003 BNB per NFT (max 10 per mint) — both draw from the same remaining pool until sold out. Total supply 4,444, one pool, no treasury. Each NFT minted also grants 1,000 RAIL automatically.
